The Box Office Report

More than just Movies

Movies are a major part of entertainment and popular culture. They bring stories to life, introduce audiences to unforgettable characters, and create experiences shared around the world. Some films become more than just movies, turning into worldwide phenomena that earn billions of dollars. The Box Office Report explores the films, franchises, and genres that have made the biggest impact on the global movie industry.

Top Box Office Hits

Some of the highest-grossing movies in history have earned more than $2 billion worldwide. **Avatar (2009)** earned approximately $2.92 billion, making it one of the most successful movies ever released. **Avengers: Endgame (2019)** earned approximately $2.94 billion worldwide through its original release and re-releases. **Avatar: The Way of Water (2022)** brought in approximately $2.33 billion at the worldwide box office. **Ne Zha 2 (2025)** earned approximately $2.27 billion, becoming one of the highest-grossing animated films of all time. These movies demonstrate how powerful storytelling, impressive animation, special effects, and international audiences contribute to box office success.
