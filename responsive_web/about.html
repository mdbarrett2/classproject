The World's Highest-Grossing Films

The movie industry has produced some of the most financially successful films in entertainment history. The highest-grossing movies are ranked by the amount of money they earn through theatrical releases around the world. Some films have earned more than two billion dollars, making them worldwide box office sensations. Avatar (2009) and Avengers: Endgame (2019) are two of the most successful films ever released, with both earning close to three billion dollars worldwide. Avatar: The Way of Water continued the success of the original film with its impressive visuals and storytelling. Ne Zha 2 also became a major box office success, showing that animated films can attract enormous audiences. These movies demonstrate how films from different genres and countries can achieve extraordinary success.

Information about the Movies

Some movies have achieved incredible success at the worldwide box office, earning billions of dollars during their theatrical releases. Avengers: Endgame (2019) earned approximately $2.94 billion worldwide, making it one of the highest-grossing movies in history. Avatar (2009), directed by James Cameron, earned approximately $2.92 billion through its original release and multiple re-releases. Avatar: The Way of Water (2022), the sequel to the original Avatar, earned approximately $2.33 billion worldwide. Ne Zha 2 (2025), a Chinese animated fantasy film, earned approximately $2.27 billion, becoming one of the most successful animated movies ever released. Each of these films attracted enormous audiences and became a major success in the global entertainment industry. Their box office earnings demonstrate how movies from different genres and countries can reach extraordinary levels of success.
