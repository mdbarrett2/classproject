The Biggest Movie Franchises in the Box Office

The Franchises That Made Billions

Movie franchises have become some of the biggest moneymakers in the entertainment industry. The Marvel Cinematic Universe is the highest-grossing movie franchise in history, with approximately $35.17 billion in worldwide box office earnings. Star Wars follows with more than $10.76 billion earned across its movies. The Wizarding World, which includes the Harry Potter and Fantastic Beasts films, has generated approximately $9.69 billion worldwide. Other successful franchises include Jurassic Park, which has earned approximately $6.94 billion, and Avatar, with approximately $6.74 billion. These franchises have attracted audiences through multiple films, recognizable characters, and stories that continue across several releases. Their success demonstrates how a single movie can grow into a much larger entertainment universe.

What Makes a Franchise Successful?

One reason movie franchises become so successful is their ability to keep audiences interested over time. Many franchises build loyal fan bases who return to theaters to follow their favorite characters and stories. The Marvel Cinematic Universe is a great example because its movies connect through shared characters and storylines, eventually bringing many superheroes together in Avengers: Endgame. The Avatar franchise has also attracted audiences by creating a unique fictional world that viewers can revisit through multiple films. Star Wars has remained popular for decades by expanding its universe through movies, characters, and generations of storytelling. Successful franchises can also grow through merchandise, streaming releases, and other forms of entertainment. These factors help explain why movie franchises continue to be an important part of the global film industry.
