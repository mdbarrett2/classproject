Genre

The top four highest-grossing movies are all science fiction films. This shows how popular the science fiction genre is with audiences around the world. Their success also demonstrates that viewers enjoy movies with exciting adventures, futuristic settings, and advanced technology.

Box office Price

The top four highest-grossing movies of all time are *Avatar*, *Avengers: Endgame*, *Avatar: The Way of Water*, and *Ne Zha 2*. *Avatar* ranks first with over $2.92 billion in worldwide earnings, followed by *Avengers: Endgame* with about $2.80 billion, *Avatar: The Way of Water* with over $2.33 billion, and *Ne Zha 2* with about $2.27 billion.
