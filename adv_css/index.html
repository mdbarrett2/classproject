The highest-grossing movies of all time have earned billions of dollars worldwide. Avatar ranks among the most successful films ever released. Avengers: Endgame also earned an enormous amount of money because of its popularity with superhero fans. Avatar: The Way of Water attracted audiences with its stunning underwater scenes and visual effects. Ne Zha 2 became a major success in international box office history. Some of the highest-grossing movies in history have earned more than $2 billion worldwide. **Avatar (2009)** earned approximately $2.92 billion, making it one of the most successful movies ever released. **Avengers: Endgame (2019)** earned approximately $2.94 billion worldwide through its original release and re-releases. **Avatar: The Way of Water (2022)** brought in approximately $2.33 billion at the worldwide box office. **Ne Zha 2 (2025)** earned approximately $2.27 billion, becoming one of the highest-grossing animated films of all time. These movies demonstrate how powerful storytelling, impressive animation, special effects, and international audiences contribute to box office success.
